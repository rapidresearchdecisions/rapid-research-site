Privacy

Short version: I measure visits to improve this site, I never sell your data, and you can ask me to delete anything you've sent.

What this site measures

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits, see which pages people view, roughly where they're from (city or country), what device they use, and how they found the site. It uses Microsoft Clarity to see how people use pages, such as clicks and scrolling, so I can make the site easier to use. Both use cookies. Clarity masks what you type into fields.

What you send me

If you fill in the request form, your answers are stored in Google Forms and used only to reply to you and run your project. Payments go through Stripe; I never see your full card details.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser or use an ad blocker, and the site still works. To see or delete anything you've sent, email hello@rapid-research.com.
