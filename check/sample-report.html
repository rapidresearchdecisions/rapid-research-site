Some text is hard to read

Quick fix · minuteslocalhost:8790

3 pieces of text are below the readable contrast level. Worst: “The AI-native platform for cross-functional velocity.” at 2.05:1 (needs 4.5:1).

Why it matters: Low-contrast text is hard to read in sunlight, on cheap screens, and for anyone with weaker eyesight. It's the most common accessibility problem on the web.

Do this: Darken the text color. For example #b5b5b5 → #747474 on this background reaches 4.7:1.

2

The page isn't set up for phones

Quick fix · minuteslocalhost:8790

No viewport meta tag.

Why it matters: Phones will show a shrunken desktop page.

Do this: Add <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1"> to the head.

Backed by: [3]

3

The headline doesn't say what the product does

Quick fix · minutesHome page, first screen

Headline: “Synergize your workflow orchestration.” Subhead: “The AI-native platform for cross-functional velocity.”

Why it matters: Visitors decide in seconds whether a page is for them. Buzzwords make them guess what you do, and most won't.

Do this: Say what it does and for whom, e.g. “Turn your team's to-dos into a weekly plan, automatically.”

Backed by: [4][5]
