How much does user research cost?

At Rapid Research, from $500 for a 24-hour Signup Teardown to $12,000 for a Launch & Fundraise Review with 20 customer interviews. Agencies often charge $25,000 or more per project.

What's the difference between a UX audit and usability testing?

A UX audit (our Signup Teardown) is an expert going through your product. Usability testing (our First-Use Test) watches real people try it. The audit is faster and cheaper; testing shows where actual users struggle.

How many users do I need to test with?

Five users usually reveal the biggest problems in a single flow. Use 15 or more when you need patterns across several parts of the product or different user types.
