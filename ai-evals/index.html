What kinds of AI products do you test?

Chatbots and assistants, copilots, agents, AI search and recommendations, and anything that writes or generates content.

Why not just have our team check the answers?

Your team knows too much. They phrase questions the way the AI expects and forgive its mistakes. Customers don't.

We already have automated evals. Why add people?

An automated grader is only useful if it agrees with real people. I check that on your own data and fix the places where it doesn't.

How do I know the grades are reliable?

Each answer is graded by at least two people using the same checklist, and I report how often they agree (Cohen's kappa, for the technical folks). If they don't agree, the checklist gets fixed before you see results.

Do you need access to our data?

Just an export of sample conversations, under NDA. Remove personal details before sharing.
