What kinds of AI products do you evaluate?

Chat assistants, copilots, agents, AI search and recommendations, and content generation, in consumer and B2B products.

What does "agreement" mean, and why does it matter?

If two people score the same answer differently, your eval is measuring opinions, not quality. I train raters on a shared rubric and report agreement statistics (like Cohen's kappa), so you know the scores are reliable.

We already use an LLM as a judge. Why add humans?

An LLM judge is only as good as its match with human judgment. Calibration checks that match on your own data and fixes the prompt where it disagrees.

Do you need access to our data?

I work with exported samples under NDA. Remove or mask personal data before sharing, and I'll handle everything in line with your policies.
