FAQ

Questions

Who are the participants?

By default, I recruit people who match your target users through a vetted research panel and screen them before each session. If you'd rather hear from your existing customers, choose the "use your own users" option.

Do I need to attend any calls?

No. You fill out a short intake form, and everything else happens on my end. Questions after delivery are handled by email. If you'd like to talk before booking, a free 15-minute call is available through the consultation form.

What kinds of products do you work with?

Web and mobile apps, especially products with AI features, personalization, or personal data. Consumer and B2B are both welcome.

What exactly do I receive?

A PDF report with ranked findings, supporting user quotes, and specific recommendations. Quick Tests add session recordings; deeper packages add severity ratings, a fix roadmap, and an executive summary.

How is participant privacy handled?

Participants consent to being recorded at the start of each session. Reports use anonymized quotes, and raw recordings are not shared without participant consent.
