FAQ

Questions

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours before sharing anything confidential and I'll sign it.

When can you start?

Fast-feedback studies usually start the next business day. Interview studies start as soon as participants are booked, typically within a few days.

Who does the work?

A senior researcher with a PhD in human-AI interaction and big-tech experience runs every study personally.

Can I pay by invoice?

Yes. Pay by card or by invoice; mention your preference in the form.

Who are the participants?

People who match your target users, recruited from a vetted research panel and screened first. You can also use your own customers, at a discount.

Do I need to join any calls?

No. Everything runs by email. A free 15-minute call is optional.

What kinds of products do you work with?

Web and mobile apps, consumer or B2B, especially products with AI, personalization, or personal data.

How is participant privacy handled?

Participants consent to recording, and reports use anonymized quotes.
